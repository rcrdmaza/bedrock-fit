# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 3, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Bedrock.fit ("we," "us," or
"our") collects, uses, and discloses information obtained from users ("you") of
the website located at https://www.bedrock.fit (the "Site"). By accessing or
using the Site, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or to submit personal identifying
information to use the Site. Any values you enter into the Site's calculator are
processed within your browser and are not transmitted to or stored by us.

We and our third-party service providers automatically collect certain
information through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address,
browser type, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages viewed, and dates and
times of access.

## 3. Cookies and Advertising

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, analyze
usage, and deliver advertising.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements
based on your prior visits to the Site and other websites. Google's use of
advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on
such visits.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your Google Ads
Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads. You may also opt out of a
third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting
https://www.aboutads.info/choices/. Additional information regarding Google's
practices is available at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## 4. Analytics

The Site uses analytics services, including Google Analytics, to collect
aggregate usage data through cookies. This information is used to understand and
improve Site performance. You may opt out of Google Analytics by installing the
opt-out browser add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct,
delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object
to certain processing.

Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom: where required,
consent for non-essential and advertising cookies is obtained through a consent
management platform, and such consent may be withdrawn at any time through the
Site's cookie settings.

Residents of California: we do not sell personal information for monetary
consideration. Certain advertising-related disclosures of information may
constitute a "sale" or "sharing" under applicable California law, and you may
exercise your opt-out rights through the mechanisms described in Section 3 and
through applicable browser-based opt-out signals.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a
child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will
take appropriate steps to delete it.
